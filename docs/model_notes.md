### NASA POWER specifics (API v2.9.9)

- Endpoint: `https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/hourly/point`
- The hourly JSON returns each parameter as a dict keyed `YYYYMMDDHH` in **LST**
  (local standard time, LST = UTC + longitude/15). We convert to UTC and snap to the
  hour grid. Missing hours use fill value `-999.0` (converted to NaN).
- `PS` is returned in **kPa**; we convert to hPa for consistency with Open-Meteo/EPW.
- The EPW export (community `SB`) needs two fixes before EnergyPlus 26 will run it:
  1. the `?9?9?9?9E0?9?9...` token in data rows is the **standard EPW missing-data flag**
     (field 6) — keep it;
  2. HOLIDAYS/DAYLIGHT SAVINGS must be `Yes` and leap-day (Feb 29) records must be dropped
     (E+ 26 cannot process them), and the missing final `12/31 24:00` record must be
     appended so the file has exactly 8760 rows. See `clean_epw()` in
     `src/data/nasa_power.py`.

## 2. Fast RC model (Phase 2/3)

Single indoor-air node, explicit Euler at 10 min steps:

    m_eff·cp·dT/dt = Σ Q_cond + Q_vent + Q_int + Q_solar_in

- **Opaque surfaces — sol-air method** (ASHRAE HOF Ch.18, ISO 13790): effective outside
  temperature `T_solair = T_out + α·POA/h_o` with `h_o = 25 W/m²K` (1/R_SE). Only the
  `U/h_o` fraction of absorbed solar enters — the rest is re-radiated at the outer surface.
  This avoids the classic lumped-model overheating bias.
- **Windows:** `U·A·(T_out−T) + POA·A·SHGC` (transmitted solar enters directly).
- **Floor:** coupled to constant shallow-ground temperature (26 °C default).
- **Ventilation:** `ρ·cp·V·ACH/3600 · (T_out−T)`, ACH from config.
- **Thermal mass:** 50% of envelope mass + indoor air, cp ≈ 1000 J/kgK.
- **Solar geometry:** pvlib (sun position; Erbs split GHI→DNI/DHI; per-surface POA with
  ground albedo 0.2).
- Comfort band default 18–32 °C; comfort hours, night heat loss and solar gain kWh are the
  metrics (see `comfort_stats`).

### Validation vs EnergyPlus (brick shelter, Prayagraj 2024, full year)

| metric | RC model | EnergyPlus 26.1 |
|---|---|---|
| mean indoor | 32.1 °C | 31.7 °C |
| min indoor | 14.2 °C | 11.1 °C |
| max indoor | 49.4 °C | 54.0 °C |
| hottest-week mean | 43.4 °C | 46.8 °C |

The RC model is a fast design-sweep tool; EnergyPlus (CTF, full surface heat balance) is the
authority for final designs. Differences of ±3–5 °C at extremes are expected for a
single-node model and are documented rather than tuned away.

## 3. EnergyPlus model (Phase 3)

- IDF generated by `simulation/energyplus/make_idf.py` from the SAME config + materials as
  the RC model (single source of truth).
- Shoebox model: 6 opaque surfaces + window + door, `FullExterior` solar distribution,
  CTF heat balance, TARP/DOE-2 convection, 4 timesteps/hour, full-year RunPeriod.
- Floor modelled as exposed-to-outdoors (raised/stilt shelter). For slab-on-grade, change
  the floor's boundary condition and add ground-temperature objects.
- **E+ 26.1 field-order notes** (this version changed the schema — verified against
  `Energy+.schema.epJSON` in the install):
  - `BuildingSurface:Detailed` has `space_name` between zone and boundary condition;
  - `Site:Location` order is lat, lon, **time_zone, elevation**;
  - `Construction` objects must not have a trailing empty layer (breaks E+ 26.1);
  - `Output:SQLite` options are only `Simple` / `SimpleAndTabular`;
  - Zone `type` minimum is 1.
- Results parsed from `eplusout.sql` (ReportData ↔ Time ↔ ReportDataDictionary).

## 4. Materials database

Every row of `data/external/materials.csv` carries a `source` (ASHRAE HOF 2021, ISO 10456/
EN 13162–13164 ranges, manufacturer datasheets). Defaults: brick walls 0.2 m, RCC roof
0.12 m, concrete floor 0.1 m, single clear glazing U 5.8 / SHGC 0.82. **Before the final
submission, re-verify each value against the cited source for the exact products used.**

## 5. Known limitations / next steps

- RC model: single node, no humidity/moisture, no long-wave sky radiation, no shading.
- E+ model: no HVAC, no occupants schedule (constant 100 W), floor-to-ambient assumption.
- Validation against ERA5 and measured data (Phase 4) still pending.
- Optimisation (SciPy/Optuna) and Streamlit dashboard are Phase 6–7.
