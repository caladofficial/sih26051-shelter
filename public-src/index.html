THE MISSION

One question drives everything:
what keeps people inside the shelter comfortable?

Given a location and its real climate, we model a shelter, simulate its hourly thermal response across the harshest week of the year, and recommend the orientation, materials and insulation that keep indoor conditions inside the comfort band — passively, with zero running cost.

01

Real climate data — NASA POWER primary, Open-Meteo cross-check, EnergyPlus-ready EPW export.

02

Sourced material physics — every conductivity, density and absorptance value carries its source.

03

Fast RC thermal engine (3R2C lumped) validated against EnergyPlus 26.1.

04

End-to-end outputs — CAD export (DXF/OBJ/STL), structure reports and shelter management.
